Principal IC altitude on the ML engineering ladder. Set the org- and industry-wide ML technical North Star; author the north-star applied-ML architecture the whole ML org converges on (living diagrams, canonical APIs); influence cross-functional stakeholders up to the SVP / CTO level without direct authority; calibrate the technical bar for the ML org and multiply the effectiveness of every ML team; and act as external voice for the ML function via publications, keynotes, and open-source thought leadership. Inherits — and does not re-teach — the multi-team, portfolio-scope, single-team, and build-altitude scope owned by [`staff-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/staff-ml-engineer-learning), [`senior-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/senior-ml-engineer-learning), and [`ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/ml-engineer-learning).

> **Status**: bootstrap curriculum plan authored 2026-07-08. See [`CURRICULUM.md`](CURRICULUM.md) for the module and project plan, [`JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md`](JOB_REQUIREMENTS.md) for the requirements catalogue and cited references (26 in-window postings, four principal-scope themes at ≥ 0.60 frequency), and [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json) for the machine-readable plan. Lessons and projects will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles. Next research re-sample is due 2026-10-08.

Planned commitment: **~145 hours** across 6 modules (70 h) + 3 projects (75 h). Deliberately smaller than the staff track beneath — the principal-owned surface is the delta between "portfolio-scope multi-team leader" (owned by `staff-ml-engineer-learning`) and "org- and industry-wide leader of the ML function", not a full second catalogue.
