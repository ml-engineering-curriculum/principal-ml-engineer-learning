# The Title Map: Distinguished, Principal, Senior Principal, Sr. Distinguished

> Chapter 3 of mod-501. Prerequisite: chapter 2 (reading a principal posting). This chapter treats the *title* as a separate axis from the *scope* — the two do not line up cleanly across employers.

## Title is not the level

The verb-and-object table from chapter 1 gives you the level (scope). The word on the door of the office gives you the title. In the 2026-Q2 sample, the two axes do not correlate as tightly as a career ladder blog post might imply.

Most employers use one of two title stacks:

- **Single-rung principal stack:** `Principal ML Engineer` — one titled rung above Staff, no further named rung between Principal and the executive branch.
- **Two-rung principal stack:** `Principal ML Engineer` → `Sr. / Senior Principal ML Engineer` — two titled rungs above Staff before you hit the executive branch or a Fellow-flavoured rung above that.

A third pattern appears specifically at Capital One and a small number of large enterprises:

- **Distinguished / Sr. Distinguished stack:** `Distinguished (AI / ML) Engineer` and `Sr. Distinguished (AI / ML) Engineer` in place of, or in addition to, `Principal` / `Sr. Principal`.

The scope shift from chapter 1 stays the same across all three stacks — the verb-and-object altitude of the load-bearing sentences is what the level actually is. But the titles do not translate one-to-one across employers, so the next few sections give you a stack-by-stack map.

## Single-rung principal stack (the modal pattern)

The most common shape in the 2026-Q2 sample. One titled rung above Staff.

- **Pinterest** — `Principal Machine Learning Engineer, Content Recommendations`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Set overall technical strategy, unified technical architecture and defining a roadmap for industry-leading methodology."* Salary top: $480K. This is a canonical single-rung principal posting.
- **Instacart** — `Principal Software / ML Engineer, Ads Quality`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Define & own the Ads Quality technical strategy and roadmap, balancing between both short-term needs and long-term sustainability."* Requires PhD + 15+ years — the level is corroborated by the tenure floor.
- **Bumble** — `Principal ML Engineer, Matching and Recommendations`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Operate as a senior individual contributor, influencing technical strategy and decision-making without direct authority."*
- **Ibotta** — `Principal ML Engineer`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Act as a leading contributor, voice, and thought leader in a growing community of machine learning practitioners."*
- **Upstart** — `Principal Machine Learning Engineer`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Your work will multiply the effectiveness of every ML team at Upstart."*
- **Wiser Solutions** — `Principal ML Engineer`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Define and evolve Wiser's AI and data science technical strategy in partnership with product and business leadership."* 15+ years floor.
- **GoodRx** — `Principal ML Engineer`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Define and drive machine learning strategy across multiple domains and product areas."*
- **Palo Alto Networks, Twilio, ServiceNow, Zscaler, Autodesk, Headspace, PhysicsX, Lila Sciences, Airbnb, DoorDash, Snap** — all title as `Principal ML Engineer` (some with a specialization suffix). All single-rung.

In a single-rung stack, `Principal` is the highest titled IC rung on the engineering ladder before you either promote sideways to the executive branch (Head of AI / VP of ML / CAIO), or you stay at Principal for the rest of your engineering career. The 2026-Q2 sample overwhelmingly points at the modal outcome being *the latter* — Larson's *Staff Engineer* interviews and the StaffEng.com Distinguished / Fellow archetype describe the same thing.

## Two-rung principal stack (Senior Principal added)

Some employers add a second titled rung above Principal. The 2026-Q2 sample has one clear representative:

- **SoundCloud** — `Senior Principal ML Engineer, New York`. Load-bearing sentence: *"Define the technical North Star for ML across engineering."* The `Senior Principal` title marks that this rung sits above the standard `Principal` rung and takes the ML function's technical North Star as its object — the highest level-48-plus object in the verb-and-object table.

Roblox's `Distinguished Engineer, ML — Discovery` posting (low confidence in the sample) belongs to a parallel structure — see the next section — but functionally acts as the two-rung stack's top-of-band rung at Roblox.

When you see a two-rung principal stack, the shape is usually:

- `Principal` — the org-scope North Star for a product area, unified architecture within that area, VP-level influence.
- `Sr. / Senior Principal` — the ML function's North Star across product areas, SVP / CTO influence, cross-product-area architectural direction.

The scope shift is qualitatively the same as chapter 1 described — object altitude moves from `product area's technical direction` to `the ML function's technical direction across product areas`.

## The Distinguished / Sr. Distinguished stack

Capital One dominates the Distinguished-title postings in the 2026-Q2 sample. Five of the 26 postings are Capital One Distinguished- or Sr.-Distinguished-titled roles. This is not a coincidence — Capital One publishes a labelled Distinguished-Engineer program with a defined technical-bar body. The distinguished-title postings map onto the principal-scope objects from chapter 1 as follows:

- **Capital One — Sr. Distinguished Machine Learning Engineer, McLean / Plano.** Preferred qualification includes *"ML industry impact through conference presentations, papers, blog posts, or open source contributions."* This is the industry-influence theme (mod-506) surfacing explicitly.
- **Capital One — Sr. Distinguished Machine Learning Engineer, Remote-Eligible.** *"Drive organization-wide platform innovation."* Org-scope architectural direction.
- **Capital One — Sr. Distinguished AI Engineer, Remote-Eligible.** *"Contribute to the technical vision and the long-term roadmap of foundational AI systems."* *"Influence cross-functional stakeholders up to the SVP level."* Salary top: $392K.
- **Capital One — Distinguished AI Engineer, McLean.** *"Influence cross-functional stakeholders up to the VP level."* Cross-org influence at the VP tier, one tier below SVP.
- **Capital One — Distinguished AI Engineer, Agentic AI Platform.** *"North star platform architecture, continuously publishing and refining living diagrams and canonical APIs."* The north-star architecture theme (mod-503) named verbatim.

The Capital One Distinguished-title family collectively runs the entire principal-scope playbook. The `Sr. Distinguished` postings tend to push influence up to the SVP level and add the industry-influence preferred qualification; the `Distinguished` postings tend to cap influence at the VP level and skip the industry-influence framing.

At Roblox and a handful of other large tech employers, `Distinguished Engineer` also appears — Roblox's `Distinguished Engineer, ML — Discovery` posting fell to low confidence in the sample (SPA timeouts), but the shape is consistent.

## Fellow rung — not in this track's window

Some employers stack a `Fellow` or `Senior Fellow` rung above `Sr. Distinguished` / `Senior Principal`. StaffEng.com's Fellow-archetype interviews describe this rung: the industry-cited applied-ML architect, the multi-year technical-bet author across an entire product portfolio. In the 2026-Q2 sample this rung did not surface for ML specifically — none of the 26 postings titled it — and this track does not attempt to model it. If your career trajectory eventually crosses that rung, the mod-506 external-voice module and the ongoing north-star architecture practice from mod-503 are the closest overlap. Fellow scope is above this track's altitude and is not authored here.

## Where the market is coherent

Across all three stacks, the following pieces are stable:

- **The verb-object altitude of the load-bearing sentences** — this is the level. Titles vary; altitudes converge.
- **IC-only.** All principal-titled and distinguished-titled ML engineer postings in the sample explicitly disavow direct reports. Bumble states it verbatim (*"without direct authority"*). Others imply it by naming *"senior individual contributor"* as the identity.
- **The four principal-scope themes** (org-industry strategy, north-star architecture, cross-org influence, mentorship + technical bar). Every principal-scope posting cites at least three of the four; most cite all four.
- **Compensation top-of-band clusters $250K–$500K** across the sample (Pinterest $480K, Snap $414K, Capital One Sr. Distinguished $392K, Lila Sciences $374K, Headspace $258.7K in high-cost regions). The number is a corroborating signal, not the level definition.

## Where the market is incoherent

Across the three stacks, the following pieces are not stable:

- **The word "Principal" itself.** Some employers use it as *the* IC ceiling; some use it as the *first* level above Staff (with `Sr. Principal` or `Distinguished` above); some marketing teams sprinkle it on the front of strong Staff-scope postings to attract senior candidates.
- **The word "Distinguished".** At Capital One it names a well-defined program with an internal technical-bar body. At other employers it means "an unusually senior IC" without a labelled program. At AI labs it can name a research-track rung (Distinguished Applied Scientist) with no engineering-track counterpart at the same employer.
- **The word "Senior".** At SoundCloud `Senior Principal` names the top-of-band ML IC rung. At other employers `Senior` in front of any title just means "at the top of the previous rung's band" and reads as the second half of a two-year time-in-rung norm — not a new rung.
- **Cross-employer level mapping.** Snap L7 is not directly comparable to Capital One's Distinguished band which is not directly comparable to SoundCloud's Senior Principal band. Use the objects, not the title, to compare.

## Reading the map

For a given posting, ask three questions:

1. **What stack is this employer running — single-rung, two-rung, or Distinguished?** If you don't already know, look at the employer's own careers page for other posted rungs. If Staff, Principal, and Sr. Principal all coexist as posted titles, it's a two-rung stack. If Distinguished and Sr. Distinguished appear, it's the Distinguished stack. Otherwise assume single-rung.
2. **Which rung in that stack is this posting?** Read the load-bearing sentences and cross-check against the object altitude from chapter 1. `Principal` in a two-rung stack usually caps at product-area North Star + VP-level influence. `Sr. Principal` and `Sr. Distinguished` push to org-wide North Star + SVP / CTO influence.
3. **Does the compensation top-of-band corroborate the level?** In a single-rung stack, the top-of-band tends to sit $250K–$300K base + significant equity. In two-rung and Distinguished stacks, the top-of-band pushes higher ($400K+ base is not unusual). Use the compensation number as a corroborating datapoint, not as the level definition.

## Summary

- Three title stacks coexist in the 2026-Q2 market: single-rung Principal (modal), two-rung Principal → Sr. Principal (SoundCloud), and Distinguished → Sr. Distinguished (Capital One).
- The Fellow / Senior Fellow rung above Sr. Distinguished / Senior Principal is above this track's altitude.
- Titles vary; the verb-object altitude of the load-bearing sentences is what actually defines the level. Chapter 1's table is the authoritative reference.
- IC-only is stable across all stacks. Direct reports, budget, headcount, and board reporting are executive-track markers — chapter 4 handles that boundary.
- Compensation top-of-band is a corroborating signal, not the level definition.
