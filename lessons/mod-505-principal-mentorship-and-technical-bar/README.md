# mod-505-principal-mentorship-and-technical-bar: Principal Mentorship & Technical-Bar Calibration for the ML Org

**Estimated effort:** 12 hours

This module owns the fourth of the four principal-scope themes named in mod-501 chapter 5: **coach staff engineers (L40) and calibrate the technical bar for the ML org**. The target framings are Upstart's *"multiply the effectiveness of every ML team in the org"* (Staff / Principal ML Engineer), Bumble's *"operate as a senior individual contributor, influencing technical strategy and decision-making without direct authority"* (Principal ML Engineer, Matching and Recommendations), Ibotta's *"act as a leading contributor, voice, and thought leader in a growing community of machine learning practitioners"* (Principal ML Engineer), ServiceNow's *"set the architectural patterns the group works from. Own the hard design calls, run the design reviews, and raise the bar on agentic design"* (Principal ML Engineer), Capital One's *"lead and mentor an engineering organization and influence cross-functional stakeholders up to the SVP level"* (Sr. Distinguished AI Engineer), and DAT Freight's *"mentoring engineers, and delivering solutions whose impact scales across teams and the broader organization"* (Principal ML Engineer). Every chapter treats one clause of that composite target.

Read the chapters in order. Chapter 1 opens with the load-bearing 1:1 the principal runs: the growth conversation with a staff engineer trying to pick between principal-IC, engineering-manager, and specialist paths. Chapter 2 lifts to the org: the small number of canonical leverage moves that lift every team. Chapter 3 walks staff-plus hiring-loop calibration — the loop level above individual-interview design. Chapter 4 sets the review bar the ML org calibrates against and the escalation path from lower-level review to principal weight. Chapter 5 walks the technical-culture leverage the principal owns *without* sliding into peer-EM scope. Chapter 6 walks the sustained-signal work — the four drift modes and the personal-cadence artifact — that keeps the principal in the archetype over years.

## Learning objectives

- Coach staff engineers (L40) — not senior tech leads (that scope is owned by [`staff-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/staff-ml-engineer-learning) mod-410 exercise-04). Set the growth conversation for a staff IC deciding between principal-IC path, engineering-manager path, and specialist-track path — and be able to name what the learner would need to see before endorsing each.
- Multiply the effectiveness of every ML team in the org per Upstart's principal role definition. Identify the small number of leverage moves (a canonical review pattern, a north-star architectural revision, a bar-raising interview change, a shared eval standard) that lift the whole org rather than one team.
- Calibrate the technical bar for the ML org at staff-plus altitude: sit on staff-plus hiring loops (loop-level calibration, not just individual interviews — the staff-scope hiring loop is owned by [`staff-ml-engineer-learning`](https://github.com/ml-engineering-curriculum/staff-ml-engineer-learning) mod-410 exercise-03), decide the bar between hire / no-hire / staff-vs-principal, and coach the loop chair on running rubric-based principal-track interviews.
- Set the code / architecture review bar the ML org calibrates against: the required rigor for a portfolio-scope RFC, the required rigor for a north-star architectural revision, and the escalation path when a lower-level review needs principal weight to unblock.
- Elevate engineering culture without sliding into people-management scope — per Bumble *"operate as a senior individual contributor... without direct authority"* and Ibotta *"act as a leading contributor, voice, and thought leader in a growing community of machine learning practitioners"* (internal community). The principal owns the technical-culture leverage; the engineering-manager peer ([`ai-infra-team-lead-learning`](https://github.com/ai-infra-curriculum/ai-infra-team-lead-learning)) owns headcount and performance reviews.
- Sustain the principal signal over years: name the failure modes (drifting into IC-only work, drifting into management, drifting into keynote-only work, chasing every bet at once) and set the personal-cadence artifact — quarterly and yearly — that the principal reviews to stay in the archetype.

## Chapters

1. [`01-coaching-the-staff-engineer-growth-conversation.md`](01-coaching-the-staff-engineer-growth-conversation.md) — the L40→? growth conversation. The three paths as they operate at L48+ (principal-IC, engineering-manager, specialist). The four evidence axes the principal reads against (energy pattern, portfolio, bar-calibration behaviour, response to the ambiguous decision). The endorsement criteria per path. The conversation shape. Six failure modes.
2. [`02-org-wide-leverage-moves.md`](02-org-wide-leverage-moves.md) — the Upstart target unpacked. The three load-bearing properties (once-authored / repeatedly-used, absent-you effective, org-scope reach). The four canonical leverage-move classes: canonical review pattern (Class A), north-star architectural revision (Class B), bar-raising interview change (Class C), shared eval standard (Class D). Selection discipline (friction-count, class-diversification, named co-authors). What is *not* a leverage move. Six failure modes.
3. [`03-staff-plus-hiring-loop-calibration.md`](03-staff-plus-hiring-loop-calibration.md) — the seam with mod-410 exercise-03. The staff-plus loop structure. The four calibration decisions (hire / no-hire from ratings shape, level from verb-object, bar-clear vs. role-fit, loop-quality). The coach-the-loop-chair arc (pre-loop 1:1, silent debrief, targeted intervention, post-loop 1:1). The rubric-based principal-track interview kinds. Five failure modes.
4. [`04-review-bar-and-escalation-path.md`](04-review-bar-and-escalation-path.md) — the tiered review bar (portfolio-scope RFC vs. north-star-scope revision) and the escalation path from lower reviews to principal weight. The eight axes for portfolio-scope RFCs plus four principal-specific reads. The seven-check north-star revision review checklist. The four escalation triggers and four anti-triggers. The escalation artifact shape and the principal's four possible responses. Six failure modes.
5. [`05-elevating-culture-without-sliding-into-management.md`](05-elevating-culture-without-sliding-into-management.md) — the Bumble and Ibotta target sentences unpacked. Technical culture is what the bar rewards. The four canonical community rituals (architecture-review study group, internal ML engineering-blog, applied-relevance reading discussion, cross-team rotation). Co-authorship as the culture-writing practice. The six specific boundary lines with the peer-EM branch. Four community-side failure modes. Interaction with mod-506.
6. [`06-sustaining-the-principal-signal-over-years.md`](06-sustaining-the-principal-signal-over-years.md) — the sustained-signal work. Four drift patterns (IC-only, management, keynote-only, chasing every bet) plus two secondary (nostalgia, gatekeeper). The personal-cadence artifact — private, quarterly (2–3 pages across 7 sections) and yearly (5–10 pages across 7 sections). What the artifact is *not*. Artifact-side failure modes. The peer-principal exchange as the closest analogue to an external audit.

## Exercises

Four exercises. Each builds one of the four artifacts a principal in this seat needs to have on hand and reflects the four learning-objective clusters.

- [`exercises/exercise-01-coach-a-staff-engineer-growth-plan.md`](exercises/exercise-01-coach-a-staff-engineer-growth-plan.md) — 3h. Run the L40→? growth conversation end-to-end for one specific staff engineer. Produce the placement notes, the endorsement-criteria addendum, and the mentee-owned growth plan the conversation converges on.
- [`exercises/exercise-02-org-scope-leverage-move-identification.md`](exercises/exercise-02-org-scope-leverage-move-identification.md) — 3h. Run the friction-count sensing, apply the three-test selection discipline, and author the working titles for the two leverage moves the principal is committing to for the next fiscal year — one per canonical class.
- [`exercises/exercise-03-staff-plus-hiring-loop-calibration-review.md`](exercises/exercise-03-staff-plus-hiring-loop-calibration-review.md) — 3h. Author the loop-chair coaching plan, the rubric-based principal-track interview design, and the debrief protocol the loop will run — plus the one-page bar-raising-interview change proposal (Class C) the review will ratify.
- [`exercises/exercise-04-principal-review-bar-and-escalation-charter.md`](exercises/exercise-04-principal-review-bar-and-escalation-charter.md) — 3h. Author the tiered review-bar charter (portfolio-scope RFC rigor vs. north-star revision rigor), the escalation-and-anti-escalation criteria, and one worked escalation walkthrough end-to-end.
